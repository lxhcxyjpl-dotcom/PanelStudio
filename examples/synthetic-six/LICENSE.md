# Example figure license

The drawings in `assets/` were made for PanelStudio. They are simple synthetic plots and shapes: lines, rectangles, circles, and one short label. They are not copied from a paper, a journal, or a third-party image library.

They are released under the same MIT license as the rest of this repository. See the root `LICENSE`.

`assets/heatmap.png` is a PNG render of `assets/heatmap.svg`, produced with the app’s own SVG rasterizer. It is not a photograph.

The `.figurezip` and `synthetic-six.svg` are exports of those assets from PanelStudio. They contain the bundled Noto fonts only inside the project package, under the SIL Open Font License texts shipped in `fonts/`.
